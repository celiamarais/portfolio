Shopping filters and assortment nomenclature

Creating filters shoppers can easily understand and navigate, using their words instead of technical language.

The redesign was rooted in extensive taxonomy work and research. I collaborated with the buying, marketing and customer service teams to understand how industry specialists and customers talk about rugs, and bridge the gap between the two.

The same nomenclature powers search, navigation and merchandising - a consistent, user-friendly system aligned with our product catalogue.

A shared vocabulary for the catalogue, and filters shoppers can easily understand and navigate.
