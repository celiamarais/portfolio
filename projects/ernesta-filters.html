Ernesta Filters

Helping shoppers find the right rug among hundreds, by size, shape, color, material and feel.

Behind a simple Filters button is a lot of work in taxonomy and nomenclature. I worked with supply-side buyers, marketing and user research to define how our rugs are described, grouped and named.

Each choice narrows the results right away, and options that would lead to zero results are disabled, so shoppers never hit a dead end. Here: a 9-foot round rug, in a dark color, in wool.

The same filters run the full catalogue on desktop, with a shared vocabulary that also powers search, navigation and merchandising.

A shared vocabulary for the catalogue, and filters shoppers can trust.
