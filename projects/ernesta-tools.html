Ernesta Process Tools

Internal tools that took rote work off our team’s plate. I’m always looking for process improvements, and I welcome limitations: they push me toward adaptable workarounds. I lead with empathy for the people doing the work.

Cut the time to generate quotes for trade customers by half. That cut the time employees spent on a rote task, so they could focus on better attending to customers.

Tear sheets, a table-stakes tool for interior design professionals, weren’t available and had been deprioritized because of tech debt on the catalogue side. I found a way around these limitations by linking our live inventory to Figma, so we could generate dozens of quotes automatically.

The result: a system built to scale, faster workflows, more satisfied customer service.

Internal teams got tools that made their workflows faster.
