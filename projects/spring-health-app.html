Spring Health App

First and sole designer at this hypergrowth mental health startup, I helped define the product system and visual design.

A simple sign-in, and clinical intake broken into bite-sized steps. And a single dashboard, where patients can find and book a therapist, and see their care plan progress.

I also designed Moments, for lower-acuity patients who didn’t want therapy but still needed everyday support. So instead of asking them to pick a category, we asked a simple question: how are you feeling? Each answer maps to the right exercises, so they can find relief, fast.

I created a scalable, cohesive visual and product system, built for small screens.
