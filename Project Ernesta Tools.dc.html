Ernesta Tools

My Role
Product Designer, First Design Hire
Problem

Ernesta's B2B customers had no dedicated area of the site, and the design and product systems behind ernesta.com hadn't yet been created or scaled.

Approach

I designed, implemented and owned the B2B area of ernesta.com end to end, while also building the design and product system the rest of the site would grow on.

Solution

I created and scaled Ernesta's product and design system, and designed automated tools that improved internal workflows — including a way to easily generate B2B quotes, and a tool to generate spec sheets directly from product data.

Result

The B2B experience shipped with a system behind it built to scale, and internal teams gained tools that made their workflows faster.
